Mist math

Humidifier season goes wrong in three places: the unit bought by its box photo instead of by the room's cubic meters, the tank judged by its look instead of by the refill rhythm it forces, and the wick discovered dead in January instead of counted out in October. Mist math turns the room into the mist, turns the mist into the tank's refill rhythm, and turns the hours into the wick's last day. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Mist plan - the room area and the ceiling become the milliliters a day the air is asking for.
  • Tank plan - the milliliters against the tank size become the refill rhythm you will live with.
  • Wick plan - the daily hours against the wick's labeled life become the day it dies.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: 30 mL a day per cubic meter of dry winter room and a 720 h wick - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. Distilled water and a weekly scrub beat any capacity number: a dirty tank grows its own weather. Keep the room near a labeled 40-50%; past that, the windows and the walls pay for it.
